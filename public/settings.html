About the estimates on this dashboard

Hattrick doesn't publish its training-speed or transfer-value formulas, and CHPP doesn't expose a player's fractional progress toward their next skill level. Rather than guess at Hattrick's internal math, Coach's Office estimates training ETAs from this app's own tracked history for each player, and shows transfer value as a transparent, order-of-magnitude estimate based on TSI and age. Both are always shown as a range with a ~, never a confident single number — treat them as a starting point for your own judgement.
